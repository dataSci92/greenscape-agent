## 📖 About The Project

**Greenscape Pro - Quote Accelerator** is an AI-powered web application that helps landscaping companies generate professional proposals in minutes instead of days.

> **The Problem:** Landscaping companies lose 35-40% of qualified leads because proposals take 6-9 days to generate.
>
> **The Solution:** This tool turns site walk notes into complete, professional proposals in under 2 hours.

### 🎯 Why This Matters

| Metric | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Quote Generation Time | 6-9 Days | ⚡ 2 Hours |
| Lost Leads (35-40%) | 52 Deals/Year | 🚀 Recovered |
| Annual Revenue Impact | -$1.47M | 💰 +$1.9M |

## ✨ Features

### 📝 **Quote Generation**
- Instant proposal generation from site notes
- Professional scope narrative in Marcus's voice
- Auto-calculated line items with pricing
- Total cost breakdown

### 🎨 **Modern UI/UX**
- Beautiful dark theme with green gradients
- Glass-morphism design
- Smooth animations
- Fully responsive (Mobile + Desktop)

### 📄 **Multi-Page Layout**
- **Home:** Proposal generator with stats
- **About:** Company story & team
- **Projects:** Portfolio showcase
- **Contact:** Contact form with info

### ⚡ **AI Integration**
- Mock AI response (ready for Claude/OpenAI)
- Realistic proposal generation
- Context-aware line item selection
